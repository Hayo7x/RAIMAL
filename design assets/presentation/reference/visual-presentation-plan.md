## Evidence boundaries

- Model execution is real, but its 480 input rows are synthetic. Display that distinction on every RAIMAL result slide.
- The top anomaly score is S11-R4 (0.688), whereas the only eligible task is S02-R3 (0.656). The higher score is not greater diagnosed severity. Show the model's ranking and the gate-selected task separately.
- Queue order in the current engine follows sector grouping, not a learned clinical/agronomic severity scale. Use “anomaly score” and “inspection priority,” not a validated severity claim.
- Sentinel-2 is the proposed real input. The supplied ESA images are real historical Saudi satellite images used for context, not data processed by this prototype.
- Prithvi's published figure is a separate crop/land-cover model reference with real HLS inputs and a prediction example. Its output is neither our inference nor a fault diagnosis. It remains an optional future context model, with no potato or fault class in this checkpoint.
- Market footprint: about 28,080 mapped Saudi pivot fields in 2020 from the 2023 retrospective study. This historical asset count is not a current customer count. GASTAT reports 624,000 tonnes of potatoes in 2024. Neither number is software revenue or a measured serviceable market. Qualify active potato pivots and willingness to pay with operators before a revenue TAM.
- The published potato maps demonstrate spatial yield variability, not irrigation-fault causality or recoverable yield loss.
