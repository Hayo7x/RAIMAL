This is a newly executed exploratory Isolation Forest experiment on real spectral features. Training uses 78 zone/date rows from February 8 and 18 with at least 80% accepted SCL coverage. February 28 is held out from fitting. Features: median NDVI, same-ring median minus zone NDVI, and change since the previous retained observation for that zone. 160 trees, random seed 42. Threshold is the 94th percentile of training scores (0.5637429039686479). Bars show the top five zones that exceed that threshold AND show peer deficit >0.075 or change <-0.07. Whole ranking is saved. Unusual greening can also score highly, so it is excluded from this lower-vigour candidate view. Close scores do not establish meaningfully different severity. Only three acquisitions exist, below the MVP eight-date history requirement: no automatic dispatch or fault confirmation is claimed. Map is an editable polygon figure encoding actual February 28 zone NDVI, north-up with clockwise sectors.

## Slide 7

Input: Sentinel-2 surface reflectance and quality masks, plus manually supplied field geometry and operator crop records. The engine ranks unusual feature combinations. RAIMAL adds quality checks, sufficient history, persistence, task geometry, review questions and a recorded technician finding. This boundary shows the system layer around the model. The current real-data experiment is offline; production ingestion, operator records and verified field outcomes are not implemented by this slide. The synthetic MVP demonstrates the inspection-record workflow separately. No automated control of irrigation is proposed.

## Slide 8

Official chart fetched from https://scikit-learn.org/stable/auto_examples/ensemble/plot_isolation_forest.html. It is the published decision-function example, not agricultural data or our output. Surrounding editable input/output flow is adapted from the official algorithm description, https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/outlier_detection.html#isolation-forest . Isolation Forest fits random feature/split trees. Shorter average isolation paths indicate more unusual observations. We display negative score_samples, so higher is more unusual; the official figure uses decision_function, a different display convention. This model provides anomaly scores, not named causes or calibrated severity.

## Slide 10

Scenario from problem_statement.md: 50 pivots × 4 rounds × 30 minutes gives 100 monthly decision/search hours. A 25% reduction releases 25 hours. At an assumed SAR 75/hour, this equals SAR 1,875 of capacity value. It is not observed savings, product revenue or a pricing proposal. Validate with operator logs. No water savings or yield gains are claimed.

## Slide 11

Proposed pilot from solution_statement.md. One operator, approximately 20 comparable pivots. Measure decision/search time, recommendation actionability and missed cases. Compare a frozen model with a simple sector rule and current practice. Inspect alerted and sampled unalerted zones independently; protect recall (target at most 5 percentage point loss). Extend if cloud gaps or too few independently confirmed cases prevent interpretation. Budget SAR 13,700 base plus 20% contingency (SAR 2,740): engineering 8,000; agronomy 1,800; technicians 2,400; compute/storage 500; travel 1,000. Request field boundaries, dated maintenance/crop records and inspection access.
