## Evidence boundaries

The real-data example uses Sentinel-2 L2A observations on 8, 18 and 28 February 2025, tile 38QMH. Raw sampled spectral bands, masks, STAC metadata, computed features and actual model output are retained in assets/real-sentinel. The field circle is manually approximated and its crop is unknown. The first two dates supply qualifying training zones; the last date is excluded from fitting. Only three acquisitions are available, so the eight-date operational history requirement is not met. The deck presents an initial vegetation-pattern assessment and exploratory ranking, not a confirmed irrigation fault, calibrated severity or validated accuracy.

The Prithvi figure is the authors' published training workflow and predictions, not our execution. Prithvi is optional future crop context. Isolation Forest is the actual engine used in the real-image experiment.

Market numbers are dated physical footprint/production measures, not revenue TAM. Pilot savings and budget are assumptions. Team credentials and majors come from the user; Muaath's IOAI 2025 bronze is also supported by official results. Hamza's ranking is presented as supplied by the user.
